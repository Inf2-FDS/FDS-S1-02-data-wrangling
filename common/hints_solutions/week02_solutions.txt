###week02_ex3
developed = life_expectancy[life_expectancy["Status"]=="Developed"]
developing = life_expectancy[life_expectancy["Status"]=="Developing"]
# One way of printing to 1 decimal place - it's also good to label output, along with units
print("Developing life expectancy: %2.1f years"%(developing["Life expectancy"].mean()))
print("Developed life expectancy:  %2.1f years"%(developed["Life expectancy"].mean()))
###week02_ex3_end

###week02_ex3.1
Both life expectancies are between 65 and 80, which seems a reasonable range for human life expectancy. The life expectancy is greater in developed countries than developing ones. This relationship seems reasonable, since developing countries tend to be richer and have better health care systems than developing ones.
